# Code of Conduct

This project is committed to providing a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment for all contributors.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, experiences, and backgrounds
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment of any kind
- Discriminatory comments or actions
- Disruptive behavior
- Violence or threats of violence

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
